Little Roam: rent a stroller instead of owning one

Borrow the stroller. Keep the kid.

Little Roam rents strollers two ways: for a trip, waiting where you land, or by the month at home, swapped as your child grows.

Five steps down to the beach.

  1. Say where and when.Enter your destination and travel dates. We tell you straight away if we serve it.
  2. Pick the stroller.Choose one that suits your child's age and weight, and add a rain cover or mosquito net if the place calls for it.
  3. Check the total.You see the full price and the handover arrangement before you reserve.
  4. Collect it nearby.The stroller is handed to you close to where you're staying. Use it for the whole trip.
  5. Hand it back.We collect it at the agreed time. Nothing comes home in the hold.

What we don't know yet.

Which city.
The pilot runs in one EU city. We haven't picked it.
Whether the prices hold.
€75, €55 and €85 are starting guesses. A survey of about 200 parents and 20 to 30 interviews come first.
Whether renting is greener.
Returning a stroller doesn't prove lower emissions. Deliveries and cleaning could cancel the benefit, so we're measuring before we claim it.
Who makes the strollers.
Little Roam is an independent concept from a 2016 design course. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Bugaboo or any other manufacturer.

Give itback.

Little Roam is a demo. No stock, no payments and no deliveries exist behind this page. The stroller is a rendered image, not a product for sale.
